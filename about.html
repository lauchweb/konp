About

Years of looking closely at products have shaped a way of seeing that goes beyond function — into proportion, detail, material, finish, and the experience of the whole.

A background rooted in product, together with a strong sense of vision and aesthetics, informs an approach to physical and digital products, services, and experiences. The work is about understanding what something is trying to achieve, what belongs, what does not, and how each part contributes to the whole.

Creating something people want to own, use, and live with is about more than the object itself. It is about the quality of everything surrounding it — how it is presented, received, used, and remembered. The product and the experience around it are considered together, because each shapes the other.

Immersion in the subject is essential: understanding the context, the materials, the details, and the intentions that give something its character. This creates the basis for decisions made with clarity, from the product itself through to the experience around it.

The product is the extension.
The experience is the journey.
